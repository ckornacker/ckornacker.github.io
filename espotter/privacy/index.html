eSpotter Privacy Policy

Effective 27 October 2026. This policy covers the eSpotter Android app (de.espotter), published by Christian Kornacker. Questions about it: christian.kornacker@gmail.com.

eSpotter shows public charging points in Germany with their reported availability and prices.

What the app does with your data

eSpotter has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting. We run no server, so we never receive your data.

To list chargers near a place, the app sends the place's coordinates and the search radius to MobiData BW, a public charging data service. The place is your phone's location if you tap the location button, and otherwise the address you picked.

While the eSpotter widget is on your home screen, the app asks MobiData BW for your favourite chargers' current status about every five minutes. While a watch runs, it asks for the watched charger every minute. These requests name the charger, not you. MobiData BW also sees your IP address, as any website would. The app sends no advertising ID, Android ID or other identifier.

The app does not keep your search coordinates or your phone's location. Nothing is written to storage except your favourites (below).

Address search

When you type an address, the app asks Android to find it. On some phones Android sends the text to a service run by the phone maker or Google. That is part of your phone, not of this app, and its policy applies.

What stays on your phone

Your favourite chargers: their name, location, a short description of the outlet, and its last known status. This is kept in the app's private storage and is left out of Android backups. It stays until you remove the favourite, clear the app's storage or uninstall the app.

Permissions

  • Location. Asked for only when you tap the location button, and used only while the app is open. You can search by address instead and never grant it.
  • Notifications. For charger alerts and the notice shown while a watch runs. Optional.
  • Running in the background. A watch checks the charger you chose every minute for up to six hours, only after you start it.

Who else gets data

  • MobiData BW, run by NVBW – Nahverkehrsgesellschaft Baden-Württemberg mbH, Rosensteinstraße 37B, 70191 Stuttgart, Germany, gets the requests described above. According to its privacy notice, its web server logs every request, including the IP address, the requested address (which contains the search coordinates or charger), the browser or app identifier, and the time, to keep the service running and secure, and deletes these logs after six months at the latest. NVBW decides on this processing itself.
  • The map app you choose, if you tap “Open in maps”. It receives the charger's coordinates.

We do not sell data or use it for advertising.

Security

The app talks to MobiData BW only over HTTPS. Favourites sit in the app's private storage.

Deleting your data

We hold no data about you, so there is nothing to delete on our side. To remove favourites, clear the app's storage or uninstall it. For MobiData BW's logs, contact NVBW as described in its privacy notice.

Your rights

If you are in the EU or EEA you can ask us for access to, and correction or deletion of, personal data we hold about you, and you can object to how it is used. You can withdraw location access at any time in Android settings. You can complain to a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the EU country where you live or work.

We rely on your consent for location (the system permission prompt), on providing the features you ask for when we send search coordinates and charger requests (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR), and on our interest in a working service for the IP address that any request carries (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Children

The app is meant for adults and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above. The current version is always on this page and linked from the app.
