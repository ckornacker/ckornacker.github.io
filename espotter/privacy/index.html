eSpotter Privacy Policy

Effective 27 October 2026. This policy covers the eSpotter Android app (de.espotter), published by Christian Kornacker (address in the legal notice). Questions: christian.kornacker@gmail.com.

What the app sends

eSpotter has no accounts, ads, analytics or crash reporting, and we run no server, so we never receive your data.

To list chargers near a place, the app sends the place's coordinates and the search radius to MobiData BW, a public charging data service. The place is your phone's location only if you tap the location button and allow it; otherwise it is the address you picked. To refresh favourites on the widget and chargers you watch, the app regularly asks MobiData BW for those chargers' status; these requests name the charger, not you. Like any website, MobiData BW also sees your IP address.

MobiData BW is run by NVBW – Nahverkehrsgesellschaft Baden-Württemberg mbH, which decides itself how it handles these requests; see its privacy notice.

Addresses you type are looked up by your phone's built-in address search, which on some phones uses a service of the phone maker or Google. Google Play handles downloading and updating the app; Google's privacy policy applies to both.

What stays on your phone

Only your favourite chargers, in the app's private storage, excluded from backups. Search coordinates and your location are not kept. Remove favourites in the app, by clearing the app's storage, or by uninstalling it.

The app talks to MobiData BW only over encrypted HTTPS connections.

Legal bases and your rights

Location access is based on your consent through Android's permission prompt (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw in Android settings at any time. Sending search coordinates and charger requests provides the features you use (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

We hold no data about you. You still have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction and objection, and to complain to a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the EU country where you live or work.
